# Recall Code of Conduct

## A welcoming community

Recall welcomes contributors and users regardless of background, identity,
experience, disability or language. Treat others respectfully in issues, pull
requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces. English and Arabic are
both welcome; do not shame people for language or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient, constructive and considerate. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and responsibility for mistakes. Help resolve disagreements calmly.
- Respect privacy, consent and others' intellectual property.
- Share only necessary, redacted information; do not expose private study material.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized comments, threats, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate disruption and retaliation against reporters are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report abuse** control on an offending comment, issue or user for
private reports to GitHub. See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not publish personal information or sensitive incident details in an issue.

For non-sensitive project moderation requests, contact the maintainer,
[@AbdulrahmanAhmedGit](https://github.com/AbdulrahmanAhmedGit), in the relevant project
thread or [Discussions](https://github.com/AbdulrahmanAhmedGit/Recall/discussions).
GitHub's private abuse reports go to GitHub, not directly to the maintainer;
this project does not advertise a separate private conduct-report inbox.
Security vulnerabilities have a different private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit/remove content, lock threads, warn participants or restrict
participation according to severity and repetition. Moderation should be fair and
proportionate, with care for affected people's privacy. Do not retaliate or evade
restrictions. Project moderation is best-effort and does not replace GitHub's policies.
